What to look at

Four things worth clicking through

The intent ladder

Submitting an email reveals a pricing step; choosing a plan reveals a call step. Nothing is charged. Each rung fires its own event, so intent is measured in stages rather than as a single conversion.

The second step

After the email is submitted, four optional questions appear at the foot of the page — age, city, wearable, how often the data gets checked. Dropdowns only. No free text and no questions about state.

Consent-gated tracking

Analytics and pixel scripts do not load until the banner is accepted. Events raised before that are queued in memory and replayed on acceptance, or dropped on refusal. Ad payloads carry counters, never answers.

Two languages

Every page ships EN and UK from one file, switched by ?lang=, then stored preference, then browser locale. The privacy policy carries both languages the same way.

Before going live

What the demo does not have

  1. Config keys sit at the top of every page in window.LP_CONFIG. FORMSPREE, GA_ID, FB_PIXEL and CALENDLY are placeholders — forms and analytics stay silent until they are filled.
  2. The prototypes embedded in the phone frames are the full interactive builds and sit in /prototypes. They are the heaviest asset in the repo.
  3. Pages are static and self-contained apart from Google Fonts. No build step, no bundler, no framework.
  4. Paths are relative: pages live in /pages and reach ../legal/ and ../prototypes/. Keeping that layout is the only deployment requirement.
