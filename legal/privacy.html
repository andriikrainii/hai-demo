Privacy policy

Version 1.0 · [ effective date ] · Applies to the Meridian, Keeper, Reserve and Anchor pages

These four pages are a concept test. We are checking whether a product like this is worth building, and we need a small amount of information from you to find out. This page explains exactly what we take, why, who else sees it, and how to make us delete it.

In short

  • We ask for your email, and on one page for three answers about how you have been sleeping and coping.
  • Those answers stay with us. They are never sent to Meta or Google.
  • Advertising and analytics cookies do not run until you agree to them.
  • Write to [ privacy@domain ] and we will delete everything about you. No reason needed.
  • This is not therapy, not a diagnosis, and not a medical device.

2What we collect

What you type in yourself

  • Your email address, on every page, when you join the waitlist or apply.
  • On the Reserve page, three answers about your sleep, your recovery and your tension, and the score calculated from them.
  • On the Anchor page, your name and contact details if you book an introductory call.
  • Anything else you choose to write in a form or send us by email.

What is collected automatically

  • Your IP address, browser and device type, and the pages you opened.
  • Where you arrived from, including the campaign tags in the link (utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, utm_content, utm_term).
  • Which brand page you saw, which language you chose, and which buttons you pressed.
  • Your language preference, saved in your own browser so the page opens correctly next time.

What we never collect

Payment details, identity document numbers, precise location, your contacts, or anything from your device beyond the browsing information listed above. There is nothing to pay for on these pages.

9Your rights

You can ask us to show you what we hold, correct it, delete it, stop using it, or send it to you in a portable file. You can withdraw any consent you gave. You can object to processing based on our legitimate interest.

Write to [ privacy@domain ]. We answer within 30 days and charge nothing. We may ask one question to confirm the request comes from you.

If you think we handled your data badly, you can complain — in Ukraine to the Ukrainian Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights, and in the European Union to the supervisory authority where you live.
